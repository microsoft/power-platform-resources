Prepare before submitting

Share the minimum sanitized evidence needed

Remove or replace sensitive information before upload. The review team needs representative structure and behavior, not production records or secrets.

  • No PII or personal dataDo not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, identifiers, or user records.
  • No secrets or credentialsRemove connection strings, keys, tokens, passwords, and authentication material.
  • No customer or production dataUse synthetic sample data and sanitized screenshots, exports, and logs.
  • Include review contextDescribe the outcome, architecture, known constraints, concerns, and target timeline.

Organizational sign-in required

You will be asked to sign in with your organizational ID before accessing the submission workflow. Access and submission eligibility may depend on your organization.

The portal opens only after an approved organizational submission URL is configured. Never submit PII or sensitive content.

A responsible review path

Prepare, assess, prioritize, improve

Keep the review bounded, evidence-based, and free of sensitive data from intake through remediation.

01 / SANITIZE

Remove sensitive data

Replace production content, identities, secrets, logs, and screenshots with safe examples.

02 / FRAME

State the concern

Describe the architecture, business outcome, constraints, and decisions needing scrutiny.

03 / REVIEW

Assess the evidence

Apply focused checks and capture severity, impact, confidence, and supporting evidence.

04 / RESOLVE

Close material risks

Assign actions, validate changes, record accepted risks, and retain the final report.
