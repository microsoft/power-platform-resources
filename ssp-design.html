A repeatable design loop

From uncertainty to a clear next step.

Combine design guides, supporting skills, and reference material. Keep decisions, assumptions, and evidence with the solution.

01 / FRAME

Define the outcome

State the business result, users, boundaries, constraints, and measures of success.

02 / EXPLORE

Compare patterns

Compare viable platform, data, experience, and integration choices using evidence.

03 / DECIDE

Record tradeoffs

Capture assumptions, consequences, and rejected options alongside the decision.

04 / VALIDATE

Review before build

Run an independent design check and resolve material risks before delivery starts.
